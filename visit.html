Questions people ask

What does the church believe?

We are believers in the Lord Jesus Christ. We highly treasure the Bible as God’s revelation. We hold the common faith shared by all genuine believers.

Read our statement of faith

Do I need to be a Christian to visit?

Our welcome says: “The church in Burlingame, California, warmly welcomes you.” If you have questions before you come, please ask.

What should I expect?

We meet on Sunday for the Lord’s Table Meeting, on Tuesday for a Prayer Meeting, and on Friday for a Small Group Meeting. The building has step-free access and an elevator.

Read what each meeting is or ask us what a first visit is like.
