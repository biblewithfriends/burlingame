Welcome

The church in Burlingame, California, warmly welcomes you. We are believers in the Lord Jesus Christ who have personally received Him as our Savior. He is the most excellent and enjoyable Person. We love Him and endeavor to give Him the first place in all things. We rejoice to be cleansed by the blood of Jesus, God’s Son, born again of the Father’s divine life, and filled with the Holy Spirit.

Read our full welcome

What we believe

We hold the faith which is common to all believers.

  • The Bible. The Bible is the complete divine revelation inspired word by word by God through the Holy Spirit.
  • The Triune God. God is uniquely one, yet a Trinity—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ. The Son of God, even God Himself, was incarnated to be a man by the name of Jesus Christ.
  • Christ’s Crucifixion. Christ died on the cross for our sins, shedding His blood for our redemption.
  • Christ’s Resurrection. Christ resurrected from among the dead on the third day.
  • Christ’s Ascension. Christ ascended to the right hand of God to be Lord of all.
  • Regeneration. Whenever any person repents to God and believes in the Lord Jesus Christ, he or she is regenerated (born again) and becomes a living member of the one Body of Christ.
  • Christ’s Return. Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.
