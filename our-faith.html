Our faith

We hold the faith which is common to all believers.

Who we are

The church in Burlingame, California, warmly welcomes you. We are believers in the Lord Jesus Christ who have personally received Him as our Savior. He is the most excellent and enjoyable Person. We love Him and endeavor to give Him the first place in all things. We rejoice to be cleansed by the blood of Jesus, God’s Son, born again of the Father’s divine life, and filled with the Holy Spirit.

We highly treasure the Holy Bible as God’s revelation of Himself and of His eternal purpose. We hold the common faith which is revealed in the Bible and is common to all genuine believers.

As is true of all believers in Christ, we are members of His one Body, the church. In order to practice the oneness of the Body with all the Christians in Burlingame, we meet as the church in Burlingame. We are in fellowship with thousands of local churches worldwide to express the one Body of Christ.

What we believe

  1. The Bible

    The Bible is the complete divine revelation inspired word by word by God through the Holy Spirit.

    2 Peter 1.21, 2 Timothy 3.16

  2. The Triune God

    God is uniquely one, yet a Trinity—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.

    1 Timothy 2.5, Matthew 28.19

  3. Jesus Christ

    The Son of God, even God Himself, was incarnated to be a man by the name of Jesus Christ.

    John 1.1, John 1.14

  4. Christ’s Crucifixion

    Christ died on the cross for our sins, shedding His blood for our redemption.

    1 Peter 2.24, Ephesians 1.7

  5. Christ’s Resurrection

    Christ resurrected from among the dead on the third day.

    1 Corinthians 15.4

  6. Christ’s Ascension

    Christ ascended to the right hand of God to be Lord of all.

    Acts 1.9, Acts 2.33, Acts 2.36

  7. Regeneration

    Whenever any person repents to God and believes in the Lord Jesus Christ, he or she is regenerated (born again) and becomes a living member of the one Body of Christ.

    Acts 20.21, John 3.3, Ephesians 1.22-23, Romans 12.5

  8. Christ’s Return

    Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.

    1 Thessalonians 2.19
