Our Faith

We hold the faith which is common to all believers.

Each statement below is followed by its Bible verses. Select “In plain English” for a simple explanation.

Who we are

The church in Burlingame, California, warmly welcomes you. We are believers in the Lord Jesus Christ who have personally received Him as our Savior. He is the most excellent and enjoyable Person. We love Him and endeavor to give Him the first place in all things. We rejoice to be cleansed by the blood of Jesus, God’s Son, born again of the Father’s divine life, and filled with the Holy Spirit.

We highly treasure the Holy Bible as God’s revelation of Himself and of His eternal purpose. We hold the common faith which is revealed in the Bible and is common to all genuine believers.

As is true of all believers in Christ, we are members of His one Body, the church. In order to practice the oneness of the Body with all the Christians in Burlingame, we meet as the church in Burlingame. We are in fellowship with thousands of local churches worldwide to express the one Body of Christ.

  1. 1 of 8

    The Bible

    The Bible is the complete divine revelation inspired word by word by God through the Holy Spirit.

    In plain English

    God gave us the whole Bible. The Holy Spirit moved the people who wrote it. So every word comes from God.

    Divine revelation
    Something God shows us about Himself that we could not find out on our own.
    Inspired
    Breathed out by God. God spoke His words through the people who wrote the Bible.

    Bible verses

    • 2 Peter 1.21
    • 2 Timothy 3.16
  2. 2 of 8

    The Triune God

    God is uniquely one, yet a Trinity—the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.

    In plain English

    There is only one God. This one God is the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.

    Triune
    Three-one. God is one God, and He is also the Father, the Son, and the Spirit.
    Trinity
    The word Christians use for God being Father, Son, and Spirit, yet one God.

    Bible verses

    • 1 Timothy 2.5
    • Matthew 28.19
  3. 3 of 8

    Jesus Christ

    The Son of God, even God Himself, was incarnated to be a man by the name of Jesus Christ.

    In plain English

    Jesus Christ is the Son of God. He is God Himself. He became a real man and lived on the earth.

    Incarnated
    Came in the flesh. God became a human being.

    Bible verses

    • John 1.1
    • John 1.14
  4. 4 of 8

    Christ’s Crucifixion

    Christ died on the cross for our sins, shedding His blood for our redemption.

    In plain English

    Jesus died on the cross for our sins. His blood paid the price to bring us back to God.

    Crucifixion
    Being put to death on a cross.
    Redemption
    Being bought back. Christ paid for our sins so we could belong to God again.

    Bible verses

    • 1 Peter 2.24
    • Ephesians 1.7
  5. 5 of 8

    Christ’s Resurrection

    Christ resurrected from among the dead on the third day.

    In plain English

    Jesus died, and on the third day He rose from the dead. He is alive.

    Resurrected
    Rose from death and lived again.

    Bible verses

    • 1 Corinthians 15.4
  6. 6 of 8

    Christ’s Ascension

    Christ ascended to the right hand of God to be Lord of all.

    In plain English

    After He rose, Jesus went up into heaven. God made Him Lord over everyone and everything.

    Ascended
    Went up into heaven.
    Lord
    The One who has the highest authority over all.

    Bible verses

    • Acts 1.9
    • Acts 2.33
    • Acts 2.36
  7. 7 of 8

    Regeneration

    Whenever any person repents to God and believes in the Lord Jesus Christ, he or she is regenerated (born again) and becomes a living member of the one Body of Christ.

    In plain English

    Anyone can turn to God and believe in Jesus. When they do, God gives them new life. They become part of the church, which the Bible calls the Body of Christ.

    Repents
    Turns his or her heart and mind to God.
    Regenerated
    Born again. Receiving God’s own life inside.
    Body of Christ
    All who believe in Jesus, joined to Him and to one another as one.

    Bible verses

    • Acts 20.21
    • John 3.3
    • Ephesians 1.22-23
    • Romans 12.5
  8. 8 of 8

    Christ’s Return

    Christ is coming again to receive His believers to Himself.

    In plain English

    Jesus will come back. When He comes, He will gather His believers to be with Him.

    Bible verses

    • 1 Thessalonians 2.19
