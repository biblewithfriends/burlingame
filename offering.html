Giving (Offering)

An offering is money given freely to God to support the church and its work. Here is how to give.

By check

Make your check payable to The church IN Burlingame.

Checks can be placed in the offering box or mailed to:

859 Cowan Rd, 3rd floor
Burlingame, CA 94010

Online with PayPal

You may also send your offering electronically using PayPal.

The church in Burlingame is registered in PayPal under:
thechurchinburlingame@gmail.com

Questions about giving? Send us a message.
