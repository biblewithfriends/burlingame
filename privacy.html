Privacy

This page explains what happens to the information you send us.

What we ask for

Our two forms ask for your name, how you would like us to reply, your phone number or email, and an optional message. We ask only for what we need to reply to you.

Who can see it

Your message is saved in a private list kept by the church. Only the people who reply to messages can see it. It is never shown on this website.

What we do with it

We use it only to reply to you. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

Removing your information

You can ask us to delete your message at any time. Send us a message to ask.

Other websites

Our Resources page links to other websites. Those websites have their own privacy rules. The map on our Visit and Contact pages is provided by Google Maps.
