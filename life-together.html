Life Together

The Christian life is lived with other believers. Here is how we meet, and what church life means.

How we meet

Lord’s Table Meeting

Sunday, 9:30 AM to 11:30 AM

What this means

At the Lord’s table, believers remember the Lord Jesus by eating the bread and drinking the cup. The bread and cup show His body and blood, given for us.

Prayer Meeting

Tuesday, 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM

What this means

At a prayer meeting, believers come together to pray to God, for one another and for His purpose.

Small Group Meeting

Friday, 6:30 PM to 9:00 PM

What this means

In a small group, a few believers meet together to read the Bible, pray, and care for one another.

The Christian life together

About the Christian life

These describe the Christian life in general, as the Bible presents it.

Gathering with other believers

The Bible tells Christians not to stop meeting together. When we gather, we encourage one another.

Growing in the knowledge of Christ

Christians keep coming to know the Lord Jesus more. We learn of Him through the Bible and through one another.

Reading and enjoying the Bible

The Bible is God’s word to us. Reading it together, slowly and often, feeds our faith.

Prayer and fellowship

Prayer is talking with God. Fellowship means sharing our life in Christ with other believers.

Connecting with other local churches

Believers in other cities meet as local churches too. We are in fellowship with them.
